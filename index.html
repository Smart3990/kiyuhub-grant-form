What's a V1 MVP, Really?

MVP stands for Minimum Viable Product, the simplest version of your app that actually works and solves one core problem. No extra features, just enough to prove people want it. Instagram's MVP was photos, likes, and comments. Uber's was book a ride, pay, and the driver shows up.

V1 stands for Version 1, the first real build we put in front of the public. Not a sketch, not a prototype. A working product people can actually download and use.

Put together, a V1 MVP is the first working version of your product, built with only the features you truly need to launch.

This approach gets you live in 2 to 3 months instead of a year. It lets you test with real users and find out if people actually want it before you spend more. And it keeps your budget focused on what's essential to start, which is exactly why we cover 40% of the cost.

Once V1 is live and you've got real feedback, and hopefully revenue, that's when we build V2 with everything else.
